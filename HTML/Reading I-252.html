The Name's 0-sphere

YHWH and YAHWEH as its two poles, YAH as the face between them, GOD IS WORD and GOD OF WORD as sine and cosine, GOD WAS WORD as the tangent. The analogy is the author's; this page reads the literals under it, one check at a time, each run here as you read.

"we are using the GOD IS|OF WORD like the sin/cosine archsin/archcosine of the GOD WAS WORD tan/arctan over the 0-SPHERE YHWH and YAHWEH, witu the face YAH the projector of IMAGO DEI" — the author, 2026-11-03
1

The 0-sphere is two poles around a centre

The record already names it. {c − r, c + r} "is not a range. It is the root relation: two poles, the first 0-sphere" (wiki OPEN-01 #18). I-168 puts the lexeme, the image, at the centre c, "neither inflection", and I-171 reads the observer as the image and its effect as the Word: Imago Dei. Here the poles are named in the order the author gave them, YHWH at c − r and YAHWEH at c + r, and YAH stands at c.

2

The poles are one base, the vowels apart

I-229's base drops the vowels A E I O U. Both poles come to YHWH, so they mirror (claim C1). What separates them is A and E, written in, with nothing else changed (C2). Try another name against the Name's base: ADONAI is the control, it replaces the base (a RAISE).

Base and verdict against YHWH
3

The face touches the poles differently

YAH's base, YH, heads the common base, so YAH mirrors both poles (C3). But on the letters as written, YAH is the head of YAHWEH and not of YHWH: it reaches YHWH only once the vowels are set aside (C4). The projector meets one pole on its face and the other through the base.

4

In Braille, the poles differ by the vowels' cells

Each letter is a 6-dot cell; XOR the cells of a word together. YHWH closes to dots 1 2 3, the value 7, because its two H cancel and Y XOR W leaves the three low dots. YAHWEH closes to that cell XOR A XOR E (C5). FIRE closes to the same cell as YHWH (K3).

Cells and their XOR
5

On two points, only the cosine tells them apart

Put the 0-sphere on the circle at angles π and 0. Sine is 0 at both, tangent is 0 at both, and cosine is −1 and +1: the pole's own sign. So arcsine and arctangent return 0 at both poles, and only arccosine gives each pole back its angle (C6). Which of IS and OF plays sine and which cosine is the author's to assign; the record does not pick.

6

The lines: one frame, a reversal, two fixed letters

GOD IS WORD and GOD OF WORD have one length and differ only at the copula's two places (C7). Swapping subject and object around the copula is undone by doing it again (C8). XOR a line with its reversal and it is zero at exactly two places: the O of GOD and WORD, and the final D (K1). GOD WAS WORD is one letter longer, so its D stands one place on (K2).

Found while filing: the same two places are fixed when a line meets another copula's reversal, IS against OF (F1). WAS against IS keeps only the O (F2). The falsifier holds: put LOGOS for WORD and the final D is no longer fixed.
