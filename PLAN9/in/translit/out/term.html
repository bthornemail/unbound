The masks between witnesses

One bit per letter cell, read right to left with the Hebrew: 1 where the pair differs. An absent witness holds the empty base, so against a reading every cell differs, and two absent witnesses show no difference.

The codices against the ZIGUAROTH codex

The codex's zeroth are YHWH, YAHWEH and GOD IS|OF WORD. Each witness's bytes XOR the bytes of each zeroth, position by position, computed in Plan 9 (zdiff). Each cell is one byte: a raised dot is a flipped bit, a blank cell is caught at that zeroth. Then the witness in braille and Aegean (ztrans), one group per code point.
